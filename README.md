# Mountain Merchants · 山地商人

A geo-economic-civilizational platform reading five of China&apos;s most consequential merchant networks — Shanxi, Huizhou, Ningbo, Wenzhou, Chaoshan — as a single structural pattern: mountain (or land-thin) home base plus adjacent metropolitan demand equals merchant civilization. Bilingual (EN / 中文), dark/light, cartographic-scholarly aesthetic with EB Garamond + Manrope + JetBrains Mono on warm sepia-parchment dark with ochre, vermillion, sage, jade, and gold accents. Ten modules: hero map showing five mountain home regions paired with four capitals/ports (Beijing, Nanjing, Shanghai, Hong Kong) plus migration outflow rays; seven-row plain-vs-mountain occupational comparison table; five colored merchant-group cards (Shanxi banking, Huizhou scholar-merchant, Ningbo treaty-port modernizers, Wenzhou small-firm cluster, Chaoshan diaspora) with home/metro pairings and stats; three Shanxi deep-dive cards (Open-Centered Salt Law, Piaohao draft banks, pre-Ming continuity question); Wu Zetian module separating evidence (Wu Shihuo timber merchant in Bingzhou per Tang dynastic histories) from interpretation (structural conditions for Tang-Shanxi commerce) from hypothesis (continuous Tang-to-Qing lineage continuity, properly contested); four Huizhou cards (geography, salt, lineage temples, scholar-merchant fusion); four coastal cards (Ningbo modern banking, Wenzhou cluster, Shanghai 1843 effect, why-different specializations); four Chaoshan-HK cards (rice-to-conglomerate, HK as financial bridge, lineage capital, Teochew language as institutional advantage); four global-parallel cards (Armenian, Jewish, Lebanese, Swiss alpine); urban-gravity simulator (5 historical pairs × 4 levers × 6 outcomes); four modern-continuation cards (Wenzhou-Prato, Yiwu, Hokkien Southeast Asia, platform era); AI Geographer with five canned multi-paragraph responses and free-text heuristic fallback; explicit guardrails section refusing ethnic essentialism, conflation of evidence with hypothesis, geographic determinism, and erasure of human cost.

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript. No build step. Bilingual via `[data-lang]` + CSS attribute selectors. Light/dark via `[data-theme]`. EB Garamond display, Manrope body, JetBrains Mono mono. All diagrams inline SVG. JS uses template literals throughout — verified with `node --check`.
